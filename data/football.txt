Lionel Messi was born on June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Argentina, and is widely regarded as one of the greatest football players in history. 

He joined FC Barcelona’s youth academy at a young age and became the club’s all-time leading goal scorer. 

Throughout his career, Messi has participated in several major tournaments, including the FIFA World Cup, Copa América, and UEFA Champions League. 

He won the FIFA World Cup with Argentina in 2022 and also secured Copa América titles. 

Messi holds numerous records, such as winning the highest number of Ballon d’Or awards and scoring hundreds of goals for both club and country. 

His achievements include multiple league titles, Champions League trophies, and international championships. 

Messi has represented teams such as Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain, Inter Miami, and the Argentina national team. 

As a forward and playmaker, his role in football is to score goals, create opportunities for teammates, and control attacking gameplay. 

Some of his most famous matches include the 2022 FIFA World Cup Final between Argentina and France and the 2011 UEFA Champions League Final against Manchester United.


Cristiano Ronaldo was born on February 5, 1985, in Madeira, Portugal, and became one of the most successful footballers in the world through his dedication and goal-scoring ability. 

He started his professional career at Sporting CP before playing for famous clubs such as Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus, and Al Nassr. 

Ronaldo has taken part in major tournaments like the FIFA World Cup, UEFA European Championship, and UEFA Champions League. 

He helped Portugal win the UEFA Euro 2016 and the UEFA Nations League. 

Ronaldo holds several records, including being one of the highest goal scorers in football history and the player with the most goals in UEFA Champions League competitions. 

His achievements include multiple Ballon d’Or awards, league titles in different countries, and international trophies. 

Ronaldo represents the Portugal national team and is known for his leadership qualities. 

As a forward and striker, his main responsibility is scoring goals, heading crosses, and leading attacks. 

Some of his famous matches include Portugal’s victory in the UEFA Euro 2016 Final and Real Madrid’s memorable Champions League finals.


Pelé, whose real name was Edson Arantes do Nascimento, was born in Brazil in 1940 and is remembered as one of the greatest legends in football history. 

He gained worldwide fame for his extraordinary skills, goal-scoring ability, and contribution to Brazilian football. 

Pelé participated in several FIFA World Cup tournaments and became the only football player to win three World Cups in 1958, 1962, and 1970. 

During his career, he played mainly for Santos FC and later for the New York Cosmos. 

Pelé scored more than one thousand goals in his football career, making him one of the highest goal scorers of all time. 

His achievements include being named FIFA Player of the Century and inspiring millions of football fans across the world. 

As a forward, his role was to score goals and create attacking opportunities for his team. 

Some of his most famous matches include the 1958 FIFA World Cup Final against Sweden and the 1970 FIFA World Cup Final against Italy, where Brazil displayed one of the greatest performances in football history.


Zinedine Zidane was born in France in 1972 and became famous for his excellent ball control, passing accuracy, and leadership on the football field.

He earned several records and awards during his career, including the FIFA World Player of the Year award multiple times.

As an attacking midfielder, his role was to create goal-scoring opportunities, control the midfield, and support both attack and defense.
